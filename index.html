// 00 The engine

Eight models. One decision every 2 seconds.

Every number ODIN can measure, it measures on your own account: the pool's payout delay, the price cut NiceHash accepts, the fill rate at each price, the dip hours, and the regime the market is in. Every number it has to estimate carries its uncertainty into the decision.

01 · HASHVALUE

Live income per EH/s

86400·R / (2³²·D) · (1−fee) · k

Difficulty and real block rewards, fees included, net of pool and buyer fees.

02 · CALIBRATION

Checked against your pool

k = ΔCredits / ΔModel(t − lag)

Compares what the pool paid with what the model expected, after the payout delay ODIN measures itself.

03 · FORECAST

Retarget ± σ

σ = (1+c)(B/2016)√(1/n + 1/B)

Next difficulty change with its error bar. The bid ceiling glides down ahead of it.

04 · PACING

Budget-aware parking

argmax f·L·(δI − (1+μ)p)

Park price solved for the money you have and how long it must last, by Lagrangian duality.

05 · DIP SPEED

Spend into the dip

L = L₀ + ΔL·clamp((m−m*)/2σ)

Speed climbs only when the margin clears the model's own error band. Changing speed is free.

06 · CUSUM

Change-point alarm

S⁺ = max(0, S⁺ + z − k) > h

Detects when the market jumps to a new level and down-weights stale history immediately.

07 · FILL CURVE

How much really fills

q(gap) = isotonic( served / limit )

Learns the share of each order served at each distance from the clearing price.

08 · VALIDATION

Walk-forward tuning

replay · no look-ahead · 2 halves

Replays your own recorded market, and adopts a setting only if it wins out of sample.

Live simulations on synthetic data, running the same algorithms ODIN runs on your account.

// 02 How ODIN decides

Bid only when it pays.

01

Read the book

Rates every price level on speed, miners, volume, fill and stability.

02

Price the hash

Works out what 1 EH/s earns now and after the next retarget, then corrects it against your pool's real credits.

03

Lock, park or pace

Locks a level once it holds. Above break-even it parks where your budget earns the most, and speeds up when a dip opens.

04

Protect capital

Hard price ceiling, step-limited raises, chase detection and daily kill switches run the whole time.

// 05 Security

Your keys never leave your machine.

Local secrets

API keys live in one settings file that only your Windows account can read. ODIN talks to NiceHash directly.

No withdrawals

Use a NiceHash key with withdrawals turned off. ODIN never needs to move coins out of your account.

Watch-only first

Every install starts in watch-only mode. Nothing trades until you switch it on yourself.

Verified builds

Programs are built by GitHub Actions. Each release ships SHA256 checksums; the Linux installer refuses a mismatch.

Tamper-evident audit ledger

Every decision is written as a SHA-256 hash chain. odin --verify-audit finds any edited, deleted or reordered line.

Offline licences

Ed25519-signed licence files, bound to one machine or a whole site and checked locally. Air-gapped sites never phone home.

// 06 Enterprise · farms, hosting sites, desks

Built for the server rack too.

Native Linux services, offline licensing, an auditable decision history and a paid pilot that proves the numbers on your own allocation before you commit.

Linux x86_64

Ubuntu 22.04+ and Debian 12+. Sandboxed systemd services, a no-login service account and root-only keys.

Offline licences

Signed .lic files: machine-bound or site-wide, with optional expiry. No licence server needed.

Audit ledger

Hash-chained JSON lines for every decision. Feed it to Loki, Splunk or Elastic.

Published network list

Outbound HTTPS to a short list of hosts. Nothing listens on the network; HUGINN uses loopback plus Tailscale.
